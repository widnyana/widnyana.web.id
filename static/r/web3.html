Certifications

The Web3 cert landscape is immature — no single credential functions as a hard HR filter yet. Verifiable track record (Immunefi payouts, CodeHawks rankings, named audit reports) carries more weight than any cert. That said, some credentials are worth the time.

Qualified Web3 Signer
Cyfrin Updraft
must-have
Proctored exam covering transaction verification, calldata review, and signing OPSEC. Launched 2025 by the most credible practitioner org in the space. Directly maps to phases 1–3 of this roadmap — key management, multisig ops, and signer security.
proctored exam 2025 launch free course updraft.cyfrin.io
Smart Contract Security & Auditing
Cyfrin Updraft
must-have
Free course + completion cert. 24+ hrs covering Solidity auditing, fuzzing, invariant testing, formal verification. Instructors from ex-OpenZeppelin and Trail of Bits. Covers the attacker mindset directly applicable to threat modeling in phases 2–3.
free 24+ hrs content 5 real audits updraft.cyfrin.io/courses/security
SSCAC — Solidity Smart Contract Auditor
Hashlock
good signal
Practitioner-level proctored exam where you audit a live codebase. Built by auditors behind high-profile DeFi projects. More rigorous than most certs — finish by auditing real code, not answering MCQ. Recognized as a "global gold standard" candidate, growing recognition.
live codebase exam proctored DeFi-focused hashlock.com.au
OSCP+ (PEN-200)
OffSec
good signal
Not Web3-specific, but still the strongest general infosec signal for anyone positioning as security-first. As of Nov 2024 it's OSCP+: a 3-year renewable credential (+ lifetime OSCP). Enhanced AD focus. Removes all doubt about offensive fundamentals. Recommended only if you don't already hold it.
gold standard 3-yr renewable hands-on exam ~$1,499 USD offsec.com
Immunefi Verified Finding

best proof-of-work
Not a certification — a verifiable on-chain track record. One confirmed medium or high finding on Immunefi outweighs any cert on a protocol security resume. The leaderboard rank is the industry's de facto credential. $162M+ in bounties available. Target first finding within 12 months of completing phase 2.

Track record > certifications in this space

Web3 protocol teams care about verifiable proof of work, not paper. The following carry more hiring signal than any certification:

  • A named finding in a public audit report (Cyfrin, Sherlock, Code4rena)
  • A confirmed Immunefi payout, any severity
  • A CodeHawks contest ranking in the top 10
  • An open-source Forta agent or detection rule with real usage
  • A documented key ceremony or multisig deployment with a named protocol
